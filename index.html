Your Supplies

Welcome to PAPTrack

A tracker for the kit you sleep with: every mask, cushion, filter, hose and chamber on its own schedule — what to wash today and what to wash this week, how long each part has left before it should be replaced, and how many spares you have, so an order goes in before you run out rather than after.

Everything you enter stays in this browser. Nothing is uploaded unless you sign in, which syncs it privately to your own account.

Start Fresh opens the Add Supply form with nothing set up, so you build the list yourself — the presets in it carry the usual cleaning and replacement schedules for each part.

Load Mask Kit sets up a full-face kit in one press: mask, headgear, tubing, filters, full-face cushions, humidifier chamber and distilled water, each on its standard schedule. Rename, add or delete any of them.

Load Nasal Kit is the same list with nasal cushions and pillows in place of the mask and its full-face cushions.

🔒 Private — stored in this browser; sign in to sync your own devices. No account required to use the app.

Back Up & Restore

One JSON file holding every supply, its schedule and its spares count — a copy you keep, independent of this browser and of any Google account. With no account needed to use PAPTrack, clearing your browser data is the one thing that will lose it.

Or take the same data to a spreadsheet. CSV is a one-way door — it's for reading and charting, not for restoring, which is what the JSON file above is for.

Restoring replaces every supply in this browser. You're told what the file holds and asked to confirm first — and if you're signed in, the restored copy becomes the one your other devices get.
