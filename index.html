Back Up & Restore

One JSON file holding every supply, its schedule and its spares count — a copy you keep, independent of this browser and of any Google account. With no account needed to use PAPTrack, clearing your browser data is the one thing that will lose it.

Restoring replaces every supply in this browser. You're told what the file holds and asked to confirm first — and if you're signed in, the restored copy becomes the one your other devices get.
